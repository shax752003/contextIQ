To facilitate the annotation process, a custom labeling tool
was developed. It runs locally to preserve data privacy and
provides a user-friendly interface. The tool is adaptable to a
wide range of NER tasks and allows researchers to customize
labels for different domains by adding or modifying tags.
To assess the practical value of the dataset, transformer-
based models such as BERT and DistiIBERT were fine-tuned
on the annotated requirement specifications. The highest-
performing model reached an F1-score of 0.95, confirming
the dataset’s ability to support accurate identification of key
components in manufacturing systems.
This combination of human expertise and Al-based augmen-
tation provides a robust foundation for training Al systems
to interpret manufacturing behaviors from text. AutoFactory
contributes to ongoing research efforts aimed at enabling in-
telligent systems to assist in generating control code from
requirement specifications, reducing reliance on manual pro-
gramming in industrial automation.

The dataset was created by manually drafting baseline requirement specifications
for manufacturing systems. These specifications were expanded into multiple
variations using large language models (ChatGPT Pro, Claude Pro, and Mistral Pro).
After expert validation, each specification was formally annotated using a custom
labeling tool with BIO tags (Begin, Inside, Outside) for Named Entity Recognition
(NER).
